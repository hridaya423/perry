# Perry architecture cleanup: shared brief

You are one of five parallel agents, each owning one area of the Perry codebase. Perry is a macOS Swift app (menu-bar screen companion) that is almost entirely AI-written and full of slop. A repo-wide review found it. The owner approved the cleanup in these words: "clear out all the slop in this codebase. clean everything up make it beautiful in terms of architecture. lean & nice." and "if its truly useless then just get rid of it absolute slop." 

## Goal

Make your area lean, direct and boring. Delete before you add. Collapse duplicate copies of one idea into the one shared shape, and reuse a canonical helper where the repo already has one. Behaviour stays the same unless this brief names a deliberate change.

## Rules (from AGENTS.md, binding)

- No comments. Delete every `//` and `///` comment in files you own, except: license headers, `///` on `public` declarations in packages, issue links, and a short note on an external constraint the code cannot express (OS bug, vendor quirk, protocol rule). Delete all "Phase N", "plan §x", "H7" style history narration without exception.
- No one-implementation protocols (a test fake that tests actually use counts as a second implementation), no single-call wrappers, no defensive checks for impossible states, no type-in-name names, no tests that cannot fail.
- No new third-party dependencies. Prefer stdlib and platform (Codable, withThrowingTaskGroup, etc.).
- `Perry.xcodeproj` is generated by xcodegen from `project.yml`; never edit it. New or deleted files under `App/` or `Packages/` are picked up automatically.
- Do NOT build, test or run anything on this machine (the MacBook). Do not ssh to the mini. The integrator builds and tests on the mini after merging. So you must be careful: grep every caller of anything you rename, delete or re-shape, across `App/`, `AppTests/`, `Packages/*/Sources`, `Packages/*/Tests`, `scripts/` before changing it.
- Never commit `.devin/`, `.audit/`, `build/`. Never `git push`.

## File ownership

You own the files listed in your task. Edit other files ONLY for the minimum call-site change your refactor forces (a renamed method, a changed init). Keep such edits mechanical and mention each one in your report. Do not refactor or de-comment files you do not own. In particular `App/AppController.swift` and `App/AppSubsystems.swift` belong to a later composition pass: touch them only to fix call sites.

Tests: update or delete tests in `AppTests/` and `Packages/*/Tests` that cover your area. Delete tests that pin internals you removed. Keep tests of real behaviour, and adjust them to the new shape.

## How to work

1. Read your area's review findings (path in your task) and the files in full before cutting. The findings are leads, not orders: verify each one by reading. Some estimates are guesses.
2. Work in small, coherent commits on your worktree branch, each with a plain message saying what got simpler. End every commit message with the line: `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`
3. Re-read your diff before finishing. Check every changed symbol with grep for leftover callers. Swift is unforgiving: watch access levels, `@MainActor` isolation, `Sendable`, exhaustive switches, default arguments that callers relied on.

## Report (your final message, under 500 words)

- Commits (sha + subject).
- Net lines: `git diff --shortstat <base>..HEAD` split into prod vs tests.
- What collapsed into what (the shape now).
- Every edit you made outside your owned files.
- Anything you chose NOT to do and why, and any risk the integrator's build should look at first.
