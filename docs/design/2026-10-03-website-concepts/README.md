# Perry website concepts

Four speculative website directions, 3 October 2026. Generated with built-in imagegen and visually inspected. Full generation prompts are saved in `prompts.json`.

Inspected the current website at `/Users/hridyaagrawal/honey/perry-website` in the browser and the approved paper onboarding in this repository. Preserve the current site's oversized type, sparse composition and sense of a character inhabiting the computer. Use the approved round-headed ink mascot, with two eyes and no mouth.

1. Off the page: Perry peels the website open to reveal the desktop beneath.
2. The pop-up desktop: a notebook unfolds into a working paper desktop.
3. One line away: the wordmark's ink becomes the mascot's path and interface annotations.
4. The night shift: a small paper theatre inside the notch, rendered in charcoal and ivory.

These are visual explorations, not implemented pages or claims of shipped interactions. The existing website and app code remain untouched.

## Off the page

![Off the page](01-off-the-page.png)

The closest successor to the current site's huge wordmark and dimensional hero. A visitor drags the page corner; Perry braces against its resistance and helps peel it back. Underneath is a contained desktop demo. As the visitor reaches the demo, Perry crosses the paper edge and points at the next control. A static open fold replaces this movement with reduced motion.

Dark adaptation: graphite stock, ivory printed type and mascot, with the desktop revealed under the curl. Keep paper-edge highlights bright enough to explain its geometry.

## The pop-up desktop

![The pop-up desktop](02-pop-up-desktop.png)

The strongest material expression of the notebook idea. Opening the book raises one desktop scene. Ask, Guide and Do change what Perry does within that same scene: answer beside a file, indicate its destination, or carry it there. Let visitors control the demonstration; keep the main CTA available throughout. On mobile, show one open scene without scroll-dependent unfolding.

Dark adaptation: a charcoal book with pale paper structures and an ivory-ink mascot. Reduce the detailed paper grain at phone size.

## One line away

![One line away](03-one-line-away.png)

The most reusable identity system. The wordmark's final stroke travels through the page, becomes a guide annotation, and ends at Perry. A single deliberate tug changes the drawn interface into a working demonstration. Use the same ink behaviour for selected navigation, focus indications and section transitions, while leaving body text conventionally typeset.

Dark adaptation: ivory ink on black sketch paper, restrained yellow highlight. Keep the ink path static under reduced motion. The render shows a connected brush stroke rather than a mathematically continuous drawing; continuity would need deliberate artwork in production.

## The night shift

![The night shift](04-the-night-shift.png)

The most atmospheric direction. The notch becomes a small paper room. Switching the theme changes the room's lighting as well as the page stock. On a visitor's explicit demo action, Perry carries a file across the slit, then settles at its edge. Avoid implying that the website can access local files or that tasks run without permission.

Light adaptation: ivory paper with a recessed graphite room and a black-ink mascot visible at its edge. Keep the same composition across modes.

## Selection

Lead with Off the page. Its reveal connects the paper identity to screen-aware assistance immediately. One line away supplies the clearest supporting visual language. The night shift is a possible dark-mode treatment; it should be reconciled with the chosen hero before implementation rather than added as a competing scene.

Visual inspection: all four preserve the mascot's two-eye, mouthless identity and readable headline/CTA hierarchy. The pop-up render adds an unrequested handwritten slogan; remove it if developing that direction. The night scene adds a lamp and mug, which suit the mood but are optional. The paper grain in concepts one and four should be quieter in production. These are desktop concept images; mobile layouts, interactive behaviour and paired theme renders remain unbuilt.
