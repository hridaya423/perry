# Four new Perry encounters

Fifth exploration, 3 October 2026. Four concepts generated and visually inspected. The fourth was refined to remove the duplicated negative-space counter.

The user strongly liked round four and requested four new concepts. Preserve its monumental graphic scale, living ink, tiny expressive companion and product invitation. Explore distinct mechanisms: removing a word as an ink ribbon, stretching the Command symbol, untangling ink into a guide line, and carrying the negative space from the wordmark.

Built-in imagegen. Prompts saved in `prompts.json`. These are static visual concepts with proposed website demonstrations, not implemented pages.

## The living redaction

![The living redaction](01-the-living-redaction.png)

Perry pulls the busywork out of the headline as a flexible ink ribbon. On activation, the middle line leaves the page, allowing the remaining lines to move together. The removed ribbon becomes the boundary of a short delegation demo. The result should make removing work physically satisfying. The render shows the removal in progress rather than its final state. Its supporting serif text is a generated deviation, not a typography decision to preserve.

## The page is a Mac

![The page is a Mac](02-the-page-is-a-mac.png)

A monumental Command symbol becomes a navigable route. Ask, Learn and Do are destinations within the loops. Perry stretches the last loop toward an exit; choosing a mode sends him and a simulated cursor down that route into the corresponding demonstration. This is the strongest colour departure and the most immediate Mac association. Final vector geometry should preserve the recognisable Command symbol beneath the lettering.

## The ink weather

![The ink weather](03-the-ink-weather.png)

A dense tangle of questions becomes a clean guide line. Choosing one question draws its line through Perry's coral ring and leads into a short screen-help example. The remaining tangle settles. The ending phrase, ah. got it., provides a clear emotional payoff. Keep the initial tangle stationary until interaction; constant scribbling could feel stressful.

## The missing piece

![The missing piece](04-the-missing-piece.png)

Perry carries the counter, the white inner space, out of the first p. It acts as a moving aperture into a contained computer-help demo. Visitors position it over the wordmark and discover what Perry can do through the opening. Clicking opens that demonstration fully. The filled p is intentional and temporary; the intro or reset should show the intact wordmark so the joke remains clear. This concept most needs motion to establish cause and effect.

## Shared interaction and theme notes

Every concept keeps acquisition visible and uses a bounded browser simulation. It does not access the visitor's real computer. Every drag interaction needs a click/tap equivalent and keyboard controls; reduced motion retains the final scene and changes state without travel. Keep a clear exit and avoid locking the pointer.

Light/dark adaptations: redaction, tangle and missing piece use ivory ink on charcoal in dark mode, retaining coral action colour. The Command concept uses deep midnight cobalt with softened ivory in dark mode. These alternate modes are proposals, not rendered or tested states.

These images establish composition and interaction premises. They do not establish responsive behaviour, animation quality, or conversion results. For the next motion study, the Command route offers the clearest mode choice; the moving counter offers the most unusual optical device.
