# Perry: a recognisable behaviour

Sixth exploration, 3 October 2026. Four images generated with built-in imagegen, saved and visually inspected.

The user liked round five except the busywork concept, and wants a memorable direction to refine. This round explores a cursor whose shadow is Perry, a question mark whose dot stands up, a handoff across a seam between you and Perry, and a cursor drawing an enormous greeting with Perry at its tip.

Built-in imagegen. Prompts are in `prompts.json`. Static concept images, with proposed contained website interactions. No application or website implementation changes.

## Your other shadow

![Your other shadow](01-your-other-shadow.png)

The cursor's expected shadow becomes an independently acting companion. Proposed behaviour: after an explicit demo activation, the simulated cursor moves, stops, and its shadow takes one extra step before looking up. It then reaches toward the next relevant control. This is visually unusual, but the generated long connected silhouette reads more like a stretched creature than a natural shadow. A refinement should reduce the connection's weight and make the detachment clearer. Do not treat the static render as a solved shadow illusion.

## The question that gets up

![The question that gets up](02-the-question-that-gets-up.png)

The dot below a question mark is Perry's head. This has the clearest connection between discovery, character and the product's Ask mode.

Proposed opening: the complete punctuation reads as a normal question mark. Its dot opens two eyes. On click or tap it rises onto its feet, pulls the question's tail into a guide line, and walks to a choice of sample questions. Selecting Where do I click opens a bounded lesson that Perry guides directly. The path can continue into Guide or Do after that first answer.

The visual should show the final friendly pose immediately under reduced motion. Ordinary questions remain accessible through native labelled controls. A quiet initial eye movement is optional; no perpetual blink loop or delayed acquisition CTA.

This is the recommended direction to refine from this round. The punctuation gives the reveal an understandable starting shape and provides a useful asking action. The current render shows the final pose; a motion study must establish the missing first two beats.

## The line between you

![The line between you](03-the-line-between-you.png)

Two huge words inhabit opposite sides of a screen seam. Perry reaches through it to meet the visitor. Moving a simulated cursor toward the seam opens it locally and reveals the same companion in a contained demonstration. This is the strongest typographic composition in the round, with a clear handoff at its centre. The generated fingers are more detailed than the approved mascot and would be simplified in final artwork.

## The cursor comet

![The cursor comet](04-the-cursor-comet.png)

Perry rides the cursor that has drawn the greeting. The proposed introduction uses one short authored path; after arrival the cursor and character settle beside the demo invitation. No freehand trails following every pointer movement. The greeting could transition into an annotation within a sample app, keeping the same ink stroke. This direction connects most directly to the approved hello onboarding, though the static render is closer to existing calligraphy concepts than the other new mechanisms.

## Theme and verification scope

All four are desktop concept images. Mobile composition, actual animation and conversion outcomes remain untested. Keep acquisition accessible before and after any demo, avoid pointer locks, and provide click/tap alternatives to gestures.

Dark adaptations: shadow becomes a pale graphite silhouette on black stock; Ask away uses deep green-black with mint-white lettering; cursor comet uses ivory ink with coral; the seam concept gets an ivory light counterpart with black lettering. These are proposed colour treatments, not additional rendered screens.
