# Perry, second exploration

Four generated and visually inspected concepts. The user preferred the living ink and dark paper theatre from round one, rejected the page peel as boring, and found the pop-up book generic. This round explores graphic rule-breaking, figure-ground illusion, translucent drawing layers and recursive geometry. No implementation changes.

Prompts are in `prompts.json`. Built-in imagegen is used for all four concepts.

## Out of line

![Out of line](01-out-of-line.png)

Perry borrows a printed notebook rule and pulls it into a rope. Proposed interaction: dragging the loose end changes its tension, and Perry adjusts his footing. The line can subsequently become a guide annotation in a contained demo. This is the strongest continuation of the user's preferred brush-lettered direction. Dark treatment: ivory rules and lettering on charcoal paper, with the same yellow margin.

## Inside the ink

![Inside the ink](02-inside-the-ink.png)

A small working world exists inside a monumental brushstroke. Proposed interaction: the brushstroke is a magnifiable part of the wordmark; entering it reveals the companion's workspace. Perry changes from ivory to black as he crosses between ink and paper. This render deliberately explores a filled silhouette, departing from the approved outline character. Dark treatment: an ivory stroke containing a graphite line drawing; retain the figure-ground reversal.

## Between the pages

![Between the pages](03-between-the-pages.png)

Cobalt ballpoint and tracing vellum form a different identity. A large faint Perry lives behind the drawing surface and offers the visitor a cursor through it. Proposed interaction: pointer movement creates shallow parallax between the drawing layers; selecting Meet Perry brings the small character into focus. The generated glove and shaded character are more illustrative than the approved mascot and would need simplification if chosen. Dark treatment: translucent graphite layers, pale blue ink and a brighter cobalt action.

## The impossible page

![The impossible page](04-the-impossible-page.png)

Three nested screens connect into a spatial shortcut. Perry reaches across their depths to pass a note. Proposed interaction: a visitor moves the note into an inner frame and it emerges beside Perry in the outer frame. The illustration was refined to restore two eyes and remove the duplicate note. The geometry remains an exploratory drawing; it is not a verified continuous optical illusion. Light treatment: graphite outlines on cool white paper with the same yellow note.

## Review

Out of line has the clearest mischievous character and the strongest complete-page composition. Inside the ink offers the most useful figure-ground device for a larger visual system. Between the pages is the most distinct color and material experiment. The impossible page is a motion premise that needs a storyboard before implementation.

All images are static desktop explorations. Described interactions and alternate themes are proposals, not implemented or rendered states. Reduced-motion versions should retain the complete final composition and support ordinary explicit demo controls. Mobile layouts would use one legible scene rather than shrinking every detail.
