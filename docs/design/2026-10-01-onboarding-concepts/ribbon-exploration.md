# Ribbon exploration

User preference: third image in generation display order, interpreted as the mint ribbon with Oh, hello. User requested more radical first-screen exploration. Four separate first-screen concepts; no implementation.

## The impossible fold

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a radically more inventive first onboarding screen for Perry, a Mac AI companion. ONE finished first screen only, landscape 3:2. The reference image is a STYLE SEED: preserve its mint sea-glass material, dark atmospheric setting and confident simplicity, but radically rethink its composition. User loves this material and wants something startling, original and exquisitely crafted. No concept board, additional screens, sidebars, forms, marketing nav or feature lists. This must be usable software, with one legible primary action and a discreet close control, not merely sculpture with text pasted on. The premise: Perry is a living ribbon that becomes the interface. Match the interface's materials and typography to that premise. Keep text extremely sparse. Do not add motivational quotes, plants, generic mascots, stock AI orbs, purple neon or unnecessary decorative objects. All text should be sharp and precisely typeset. Render a polished, plausible first-launch experience at an extraordinary art-direction level.
Direction: a single impossible fold. A vast sheet of translucent mint optical glass, thinner than paper, enters from the top-right and turns inside out into a beautifully impossible ribbon loop at centre-right. Stunning controlled refraction, visible thickness at its edges, soft caustics, occasional pearlescent spectral edge, an architectural scale to the curvature. The lower end of the SAME continuous glass ribbon sweeps across the bottom and folds into a small solid tactile tab, the primary button itself, labelled "Wake Perry". It must visibly and physically belong to the sculpture yet read instantly as clickable, with a crisp dark label and affordant raised edge. Rich graphite negative space on the left with huge beautifully controlled lightweight sans text "Something's here." and tiny supporting line "Meet Perry." A tiny mint pinpoint above the wordmark suggests life. Window frame nearly disappears; no large glass rectangle behind the scene. The fold feels impossibly graceful, tactile and alive. Art and controls one continuous object, asymmetrical, precise, audacious. No cartoon or face. Maintain generous quiet space, high contrast text and no distracting extra elements.

## The glass handshake

Direction: the first screen is an invitation to touch. In a very dark charcoal seamless native app canvas, an exquisite mint glass ribbon snakes from the far left in a slow broad wave and bends upward in the centre like a living creature listening, no face, no limbs. Its near end turns into a substantial beautifully tactile rounded glass key, seen almost front-on with slight perspective, labelled "hello" in crisp dark lowercase typography. A subtle inset return-arrow symbol at the key's right communicates you can press Return. This central key is the ONLY main action; it is physically the end of Perry's ribbon, an invitation to interact with the companion itself. Above this sculptural control is large but intimate text "Go on. Say hello." and one small line "I'm Perry." Materials are spectacular: translucent mint optical glass, polished edge highlights, subtle internal striations, caustics on a finely textured graphite floor, contrast between razor-thin ribbon and thick pressable key. Compose with monumental emptiness and editorial rigor, not a giant hero logo plus generic button. A tiny Perry wordmark upper left, close upper right, microcopy "Click or press Return" lower centre. No microphone symbols, because this is a click interaction before permissions. The physical UI should feel irresistible to press, beautiful and immediately understandable.

Direction: Perry writes its own arrival in light. One dark smoke-grey app canvas, near-borderless. Across the upper central area a single translucent mint ribbon forms a stunning continuous handwritten "hello", actual legible connected cursive lettering made from thin three-dimensional sea glass, with beautiful twisting cross-sections and self-shadowing. It should feel like a master calligrapher drew with a living strip of glass; graceful rather than balloon letters. The terminal stroke gently falls toward the bottom of the scene and curls into the fine perimeter of a compact luminous button labelled "Meet Perry". This visually connects the greeting to the action, all one material gesture. Below the glass handwriting, precise quiet native sans text "A little help, right where you are." Tiny restrained Perry wordmark and dismiss control in corners. Huge dark negative space around the gesture; soft mint caustics illuminate only a small area, no fog or diffuse green glow over everything. Spectacular typography-as-sculpture, sophisticated, personal and magical. No additional art, badges or notes. Make the button label exceptionally legible and controls plausible at desktop scale. Visually unmistakable and authored, expressive rather than corporate.
