## Shared brief

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-fidelity design concept board for Perry, a native macOS AI companion. This is an actual desktop app onboarding flow, not a website. Render four complete flat front-facing 720x520-proportioned macOS windows in an orderly 2x2 grid on a neutral presentation canvas, large high-resolution landscape image. Top row light mode: welcome left, connection right. Bottom row dark mode: the exact same two screens. Small outside-board title and row/column captions, generous gaps, no overlapping windows. All windows have understated real macOS traffic lights, fine shadows, continuous corners, restrained native SF Pro typography, 28pt headings, 14pt readable body, 36pt controls. No sidebar, navigation tabs, chat history, giant cursor, gradient blobs, purple AI glow, generic sparkle icon, feature card grid, fake analytics or dashboard.

Keep exact content and flow consistent across all four windows: Welcome screen has original crafted illustration occupying about 40% of content, heading "Meet Perry.", body "Ask a question. Learn an app. Get a hand with a task.", primary button "Get started", understated footer "Screen and microphone access come later." Connection screen has a smaller related illustration, heading "Connect your account", body "Use your ChatGPT account to start a conversation.", one primary "Connect ChatGPT" button, small secondary link "Other models", footer back affordance "Back" left and "Set up later" right. Account setup has one calm content column, not multiple equal choices. No invented privacy, security, free-tier or capability promises. Design the artwork deliberately and originally; don't copy Hey Clicky characters or branding. Art belongs within onboarding and should translate to a tiny menu bar identity, never replace the user's cursor. Both modes should look native and carefully art-directed, not mechanically inverted. This is a visual concept, not an implemented screenshot.

## 01 / Quiet native

Art direction: exceptional restraint, a Mac utility that could ship with the OS but has one recognizable signature. Chalk-white and graphite neutral surfaces, deep evergreen primary buttons in light mode and soft mint in dark. Original illustration: a fine mint line threads through two small beautifully rendered native window fragments, ending in a subtle bracket around a meaningful control. This is contextual guidance rather than a decorative swoosh; no giant pointer. Compact centered composition with deliberate whitespace, optical balance, black text and practical native controls. Connection art is two simple window fragments neatly meeting at their edges, not cables or a logo collage. Small quiet Perry wordmark. No mascot. Highest craft and absolute clarity.

## 02 / Desktop atelier

Art direction: charming illustrated software with editorial precision, a contemporary affectionate interpretation of early Macintosh desktop culture. Soft neutral white, ink-black type, existing Perry mint accent only for primary actions and small details. Welcome art is a custom small still-life collage of carefully drawn Mac objects: one blue file folder, a tiny note with a single mint pencil line, a miniature application window, arranged with beautiful spatial composition and tactile printed-paper shading. A tiny original hand-drawn black loop character with two unobtrusive eyes peeks from the note; not a cursor and not Clicky's face. Connection art uses the same character beside two neatly aligned app tiles. Crisp SF Pro controls keep it modern. Dark mode uses graphite paper and retains illustration color with dark-adapted shadows. Art must feel authored, not stock emoji, clipart or generic 3D icons.

## 03 / Small companion

Art direction: a single distinctive sculptural companion and almost invisible UI. Off-white window, near-black text, deep forest buttons, carefully lit original pebble-like folded mint ceramic creature about the size of an app icon, with a shallow fold suggesting a wing and two tiny inset eyes. Absolutely no feet, arms, cartoon grin, robot, cursor, glass arrow, balloon or generic shiny blob. Welcome illustration: the little companion rests on the corner of one small real-looking Mac application window, assisting rather than dominating. Connection illustration: the same ceramic form peeks around a small paired-window object. Generous calm space, intimate proportions, precise left-aligned content block beneath art, short native button. Dark mode charcoal with soft studio rim lighting and muted mint CTA. Design should feel like a beautiful carefully crafted Mac companion, not a children's toy. Art is small enough that the real interface remains the focus.

## 04 / Precision

Art direction: confident monochrome, product-led and utterly spare. Paper-white and carbon-black surfaces, almost-black primary buttons in light mode, pale neutral buttons in dark, restrained mint reserved for a single contextual annotation. Welcome visual is one extremely precise compact diagram of a Mac window with a tiny selected control and an elegant mint hand-drawn underline pointing out the control; nearby a small attached annotation says 'Right here'. No big cursor and no mascot. Make typography and spacing carry the design, with a wide left-aligned content column and very delicate illustration above. Connection illustration is a minimal outlined account card passing into a miniature Perry window. Real Mac window structure with premium optical alignment. Hairline rules only where useful. Avoid generic SaaS, terminal aesthetic, boxed feature lists and heavy glass. The visual signature is a small open corner-bracket around what matters.
