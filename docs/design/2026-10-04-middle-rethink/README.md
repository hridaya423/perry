## Recommendation after the first three concepts

Use the persistent stage from concept 3, the character acting from concept 2, and the object continuity from concept 1. Do not implement any of the images literally. The first two improve the mood but retain a repetitive headline-left/window-right structure. The third creates a stronger compositional foundation by keeping the work in one place.

The central rule: when the visitor moves forward, something they already recognize changes. The photo, application window, Perry and Dock should persist. The page should stop rebuilding its visual hierarchy at every section boundary.

## What should change in the actual composition

The hero's black notch retracts while Perry remains on screen. A real-looking Mac window enters the same space. The warm ink world stays around the edges; it does not compete with the action in the center.

Ask needs one focal relationship: Perry, a relevant object and a concise answer anchored to it. Marketing headings must not be placed inside Finder as if Finder is the website's page builder.

Learn keeps that object on screen. Move closer only when detail matters. One coral annotation terminates at the actual control. Perry's changing posture explains where to look and gives the interaction personality.

Do should show a visible consequence of consent. The object moves or changes, the completed result stays available to inspect, and Perry relaxes. The final reduction of the working window makes space for the Dock and the existing footer.

## Warmth is behavior

Perry looks toward the relevant detail. He leans when curious, braces during shared effort, and sits after completion. Keep a compact body and short limbs consistent with the hero. No generic waving figure attached to the bottom of every scene.

Allow quiet moments around the action. A character constantly bobbing, every window tilting and every object moving would dilute personality. Aim for anticipation, one clear action and a settled result.

## Fidelity boundary

The Mac is the recognizable environment. Its Finder icon, app icons, title bars and native controls need authoritative assets or faithful captures. The current Finder icon is constructed from an approximate SVG. This affects the credibility of the entire setting, particularly when the Dock enlarges.

Imagegen output is a composition reference, not a source for shipping Apple icons or asserting exact app UI. Generated microcopy and toolbar details require replacement. Photographic content in these concepts is a design example, not a verified Perry feature demonstration.

## Review before implementation

Choose the persistent-stage direction with the user. Then refine the one main frame at desktop, mobile and in dark mode; storyboard its entry and exit; verify native icon assets and character proportions. Only then build motion. No new framework is needed to decide the art direction.
